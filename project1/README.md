# Ecommerce Sales Analysis
## Introduction
This data is about sales records of 12 months.Analyzed products within each category,their ratings, their performances for each month.

## Dataset
Source: [Kaggle - E-Commerce Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/fahmidachowdhury/e-commerce-sales-analysis)

## Tools
- SQL

## Analysis Performed
- Total Products
- Duplicate Products
- Products where the details are missing
- Average, Minimum,Maximum price for each category
- Average review score and average review count for each category
- Product's price ranges
- Top 10 Products by total anuual sales
- Total annual sales for each category
- Month with the highest annual sales across all products
- Month with the lowest annual sales across all products
- Annual sales and average monthly sales for every product
- Products whose annual sales are above the average annual sales of all products.
- Product's Rank by annual sales within each category.
- For each category, identify the product(s) with the highest annual sales
